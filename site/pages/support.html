Signing in

I didn’t get the sign-in email

Check spam and promotions, and wait a minute. You can ask for a new one after 60 seconds. The email has a button and a 6-digit code; either works.

The sign-in link opened the browser, not the app

Type the 6-digit code from the same email into the app instead.

Your wardrobe

My clothes aren’t showing on my new phone

Make sure you’re signed in with the same account on both phones, then open Account → Sync now. On the free plan, up to 50 items sync.

How do I back up everything?

Settings → Backup → Export. It saves a single file with all your clothes and photos that you can restore any time.

Background removal didn’t work

Plain, well-lit backgrounds work best. You can turn automatic removal off in Settings → Photos.

Premium and Noha Money

I paid but Premium isn’t showing

In the app, open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases. For Noha Money purchases, sign out and back in. Still stuck? Email us with your account email.

How do I cancel?

Store subscriptions: in Google Play or iPhone Settings → Subscriptions. Noha Money purchases don’t renew. See refunds & cancellation.

Is my Noha Money from Asanoha the same here?

Yes. It’s one wallet and one history for all Asanoha products. Check it on your account page or in the app under Account.
