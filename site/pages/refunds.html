Refunds & cancellation

Last updated 10 November 2026

In short: store subscriptions are cancelled and refunded through Google Play or the App Store. Premium bought with Noha Money can be cancelled for a refund to your wallet within 7 days. Unused added money can be refunded to your UPI account.

Premium bought in the app

  • Cancel: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Wearday, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wearday. You keep Premium until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
  • Refunds: Google and Apple decide store refunds. Ask Google at play.google.com refunds, or Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can’t refund store purchases ourselves.

Premium bought with Noha Money

  • These are one-time purchases that never renew, so there’s nothing to cancel later.
  • Changed your mind? Write to us within 7 days of buying and we refund the full amount to your Noha Money wallet, in the same mix of added money and cashback you paid with. Premium time from that purchase is removed.
  • After 7 days, purchases aren’t refundable, except where the law requires or Wearday Premium didn’t work as described.

Money you added to Noha Money

  • Unused added money can be refunded to the UPI account you paid from, at any time while it’s valid. Email us from your account email with the amount. Refunds are processed within 7 working days; your bank may take a few more days to show it.
  • Cashback, referral and birthday credit are rewards and can’t be cashed out.
  • If a top-up failed but money left your account, it’s usually reversed by your bank within 48 hours. If not, send us the UPI reference (UTR) and we’ll credit or refund it.

Asanoha shop orders

Orders from asanoha.co.in follow the Asanoha terms. Refunds for cancelled shop orders paid with Noha Money go back to your wallet straight away, and show up in the same history in Wearday.

How to ask

Email orders@asanoha.co.in from your account email with what you’d like refunded. We reply within 2 working days.
