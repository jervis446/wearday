Delete your account

You can delete your Wearday account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Wearday and tap your profile icon at the top right.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

It happens straight away.

Without the app

Email orders@asanoha.co.in from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my Wearday account”. We confirm it’s you and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your wardrobe, outfits, calendar, trips and collections in the cloud
  • All your photos and outfit selfies in the cloud
  • Noha Money cashback, and Premium bought with Noha Money

What we keep, and for how long

  • Records of payments and Noha Money transactions, for 8 years, because Indian tax and accounting law requires it. They aren’t used for anything else.
  • Usage statistics linked to a random ID, for up to 12 months.
Before you delete: spend your Noha Money or ask for a refund of added money (see refunds). Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription, so cancel that in your store settings. The copy of your wardrobe on your phone stays until you uninstall the app.
