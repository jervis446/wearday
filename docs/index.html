FAQ

Good questions.

Is Wearday free?

Yes. The closet, outfits, diary and packing are free. Premium adds unlimited AI outfits, full sync, advanced stats and unlimited trips and collections.

Who can see my clothes?

Only you. Your wardrobe is stored privately in your account and synced between your phones. We don’t sell data or show ads. Read the privacy policy.

Does the AI see my photos?

No. Outfit suggestions use a text list of your clothes (type, colour, how often you wear them). Photos never leave your account.

What is Noha Money?

Noha Money is the wallet shared by Asanoha’s products. Cashback and money you add on Asanoha can also pay for Wearday Premium on the web, and every rupee shows up in one history. See the terms.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Or follow the steps on the delete account page.
