Wearday Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026: friends and sharing fits

In short: You sign in to Wearday with Apple, Google or your email. Your wardrobe (including photos) is kept on your phone and in your private cloud account so it's backed up and syncs between your devices; only you can access it. We use privacy-friendly usage statistics that you can turn off, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your account and cloud sync

You need an account to use Wearday. We store the following in your account so it can be backed up and synced to your other devices:

  • Account details: your email address, and the sign-in method you used (email code, Google or Apple). If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead.
  • Your wardrobe: clothing photos and outfit selfies, item details (name, category, colour, brand, size, price, tags, notes, status), outfits, collections, your outfit calendar, trips and packing lists.

This data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase, encrypted in transit, and protected so that only your account can read or change it. We do not look at your wardrobe or photos, except if you ask us for help and give permission, or where the law requires it.

When you sign in with Google or Apple, that company tells us your email address (and, for Apple, optionally your name); we do not receive your password or other account information.

Friends and shared fits

If you use friends and "Rate my fit", we also store:

  • Your profile: the username and name you choose. Friends, and people you have a friend request with, see them. Your email address is never shown to anyone.
  • Your friends: who you're friends with, pending requests, and anyone you've blocked.
  • Fits you share: the photo, caption and the names of the clothes you tagged. Only your current friends can see them. Photos are re-encoded on your phone before upload, which removes hidden details such as where the photo was taken.
  • Reactions and comments: your reaction and comment on a friend's fit. Only you and that fit's author see them.
  • Reports: which fit you reported and why. The author is never told who reported it.

Nobody can search for or browse other users; friends find each other only by exact username or an invite link. If a fit is reported, we may look at that fit to decide whether it breaks the community guidelines. Delete a fit at any time and it, and its reactions, are removed for everyone. Removing or blocking a friend stops them seeing your fits straight away.

Processing that happens on your device

  • Colour and category detection and background removal run on your phone (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iPhone). Photos are not uploaded for this. On Android, Google Play services may download the on-device model the first time.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone.

Purchases (Wearday Premium)

Subscriptions are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They handle your payment (card, UPI, wallet and so on); we never see your payment details. To know who has Premium, we use RevenueCat, which receives the store's purchase records (product, dates, renewal status and store transaction IDs). If you're signed in, these are linked to your random account ID so Premium works on all your devices. We store whether your account has Premium and until when.

Outfit suggestions and weather

Usage statistics

To learn which features are useful, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog (hosted in the EU): which screens are opened, and actions such as "added an item", "saved an outfit" or "created a trip", with simple counts (for example, how many items were in an outfit) and basic device information (model, operating system, app version, language). We never send photos, item names, notes, your email or your location in these events. If you are signed in, events are linked to a random account ID, not your name or email. There is no screen recording.

You can turn this off any time in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: to photograph clothes and outfit selfies, or pick them from your gallery.
  • Approximate location (optional): only for weather-aware suggestions.
  • Notifications (optional): only for the daily reminder you switch on.

Backups and sharing

When you export a backup or share an outfit image, the file goes to the app or service you choose (for example Google Drive, iCloud or WhatsApp), under that service's own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete individual items, outfits, trips or calendar entries in the app at any time; with sync on, they are deleted from your cloud account too.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account & sync → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored in the cloud, including photos, fits you shared, your reactions and your friends list. The copy on your phone is kept until you uninstall the app.
  • Signing out keeps your wardrobe on the phone and stops syncing.
  • We keep cloud data for as long as you have an account. Usage statistics are kept for up to 12 months.

Your rights

You can access, export (Settings → Backup), correct or delete your data at any time in the app. For anything else, such as a copy of the data we hold about you, contact us at the address below.

Children

Wearday is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Friends and sharing fits are only for people 13 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
