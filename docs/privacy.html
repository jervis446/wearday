Wearday Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

In short: Wearday keeps your wardrobe on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. Your photos never leave your device unless you choose to share or back them up yourself. The only thing sent over the internet is the optional Suggest an outfit request, which contains a text description of your clothes and, if you allow it, your approximate location.

What stays on your device

Everything you add to Wearday is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • clothing photos, outfit selfies and the details you enter (name, category, colour, brand, size, price, tags, notes and status);
  • outfits, collections, trips and packing lists;
  • your outfit calendar and the statistics calculated from it;
  • your settings.

We do not have a server that stores this data, and we cannot see it.

Processing that happens on your device

  • Colour and category detection. When you add a photo, the app estimates its colour, and on supported phones its category, using on-device processing (Google ML Kit). The photo is not uploaded.
  • Background removal. On supported phones, cutting the item out of its background uses Google ML Kit (Android) or Apple Vision (iOS) on the device. The photo is not uploaded. On Android, Google Play services may download the on-device model the first time it is used.
  • Reminders. Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push-notification service is used.

What is sent over the internet, and only when you ask

FeatureWhat is sentWhere it goes
Suggest an outfit A text list of the clothes in your closet (item id, name, category, colour, seasons, tags, how many times worn, date last worn), the occasion you picked and today's date. No photos. Our suggestion service on Cloudflare Workers, which asks an AI model hosted by Cloudflare (Workers AI) for an outfit.
Use today's weather (optional) Your location rounded to about 1 km, sent only if you switch this on and grant location permission. Our suggestion service, which passes the rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. Open-Meteo sees our server, not your device.

We do not store the content of these requests. Like any web service, Cloudflare processes basic request information (such as IP address and time) to deliver and protect the service, and our service keeps short-lived technical logs of errors. Cloudflare's handling of data is described in the Cloudflare privacy policy. If you don't use Suggest an outfit, the app makes no network requests to us.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: to photograph clothes and outfit selfies, or pick them from your gallery.
  • Location (optional): only for weather-aware suggestions, as described above.
  • Notifications (optional): only for the daily reminder you switch on.

You can change these at any time in your phone's settings. The app keeps working without them.

Backups and sharing

When you export a backup or share an outfit image, the file is handed to the app or service you choose (for example Google Drive, iCloud or WhatsApp). Those services handle it under their own privacy policies. Wearday does not upload backups anywhere itself.

Deleting your data

Delete individual items, outfits, trips or calendar entries in the app at any time. Uninstalling Wearday deletes all of its data from your phone. Because we hold no account or wardrobe data on a server, there is nothing else to delete. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Wearday is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
